Micro-interactions & magnetic elements

Six interaction stations: a magnetic button, a particle-burst like, a squash-and-stretch toggle, a morphing submit, an error shake, and a cascading star rating.

Why it catches attention: instant, physical feedback makes an interface feel alive — each response arrives inside the ~100ms window where cause and effect feel simultaneous. Overshoot and squash borrow real-world physics, so the eye reads the element as an object, not a pixel.

Craft notes: magnet pulls at 35% of cursor offset within a 120px radius and springs home with a lerp of 0.12/frame; pops use cubic-bezier(.34,1.56,.64,1) overshoot at 250–450ms; the submit morph runs 450ms on an emphasized curve, then draws its check via stroke-dashoffset in 400ms; the error shake is ±4px for 400ms — enough to notice, too brief to annoy.

Reduced motion: magnetism and particle bursts are disabled; state changes (like, toggle, success, error) become instant color/fill swaps, and the spinner slows rather than spins fast.
