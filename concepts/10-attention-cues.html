10Attention Cues

Attention cues & notification choreography

A mock team-inbox where you fire the events yourself and watch the interface choose its attention weapon: toast springs, badge pops, bell wiggle, pulsing unread dots, and a one-shot confetti celebration.

Why it catches attention: each cue sits at a deliberate rung on the salience ladder — a static dot whispers, a pulse hums, a wiggle taps your shoulder, a toast interrupts. Good products spend attention like a budget: the rare celebration stays special because everything else stays quiet.

Craft notes: toasts enter over 500ms on cubic-bezier(.34,1.56,.64,1) with a visible lifetime bar (hover pauses it — WCAG 2.2.2); exits take 250ms, half the entrance. Badge pops 45% past scale; the bell rings through 5 decaying swings (700ms); unread pings loop at a slow 1.8s so they don't compete with real events.

Reduced motion: toasts crossfade in place, the bell and flame hold still, pings stop looping, and confetti is skipped entirely — every state change stays visible as a color/badge change.
