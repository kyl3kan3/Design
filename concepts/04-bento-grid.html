04Bento Grid Choreography

Bento grid with hover choreography

A feature grid in the bento style — mixed tile sizes, each with its own ambient motion that intensifies on hover, plus a cursor-tracked spotlight that lights up card borders as you sweep across.

Why it catches attention: the spotlight border creates a moving point of light that follows the hand — motion + luminance contrast, the two strongest pre-attentive signals, locked to the cursor. Varied tile sizes break scanning monotony (Von Restorff effect), and hover intensification rewards exploration.

Craft notes: spotlight is a 260px radial gradient masked to a 1px border ring, fading in over 350ms; cards enter with a 90ms stagger over 650ms on cubic-bezier(.16,1,.3,1); ambient loops run slow (1.4–9s) and snap to 3–4× speed on hover; sparkline bars stagger 35ms per bar.

Reduced motion: cards render in place, the cable draws instantly, orbits/equalizer/keycaps freeze at a composed static state, and only color feedback remains on hover.
