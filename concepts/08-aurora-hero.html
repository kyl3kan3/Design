08Aurora Ambient Hero

Aurora gradient field + grain

A hero built from three drifting, heavily blurred gradient blobs under an animated film-grain overlay, with a shimmer running through the headline and a CTA ringed by a rotating conic gradient.

Why it catches attention: slow chromatic drift is ambient salience — enough change to keep peripheral vision engaged, too slow to distract from reading. Grain adds perceived texture and “expensiveness” (flat gradients read as cheap; noisy ones read as film). The one fast element — the CTA ring — inherits all the attention the background primes.

Craft notes: blobs drift on 26–38s alternate loops at 70px blur so hues mix optically; grain is an inline-SVG feTurbulence tile stepped at 3fps (steps(3)) for a projector feel; the CTA animates a registered @property --ring angle through a conic border over 4s; headline shimmer sweeps every 7s — rest is deliberate.

Reduced motion: blobs freeze into a static composition, grain stops stepping, the shimmer and pulse stop, and all copy renders immediately.
